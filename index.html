05
The A–E protocol

Five movements.
One deliberation.

A–E is the visible rhythm of the CONCLAIVE method. The user does not operate these stages manually; the system orchestrates them according to the question.

A
Assemble

Determine what the question requires. Bring the right disciplines, evidence and perspectives to the table.

B
Broaden

Expand the proposition beyond its initial assumptions. Surface constraints, possibilities and stakeholder realities.

C
Challenge

Attack the proposition. Seek contradictions, unsupported assumptions, failure modes and reasons it might be wrong.

D
Deliberate

Allow competing positions to interact. Independent voices can disagree, reconcile and test one another.

E
Emerge

Produce a defensible position — with uncertainty, unresolved questions and the human decision gate clearly preserved.

Exceptional mode
REDLINE / When the question cannot wait.

A higher-scrutiny operating mode for urgent cases: tighter escalation, compressed deliberation and immediate human intervention where required.

08
Dynamic intelligence

The room changes
with the question.

You never select “Legal Agent”, “CFO Agent” or “UX Agent”. CONCLAIVE infers which disciplines matter and assembles the appropriate seats behind the scenes.

Persistent anchors. A small leadership layer can remain present across cases for product, systems and AI-native thinking. Specialists on demand. Legal, finance, science, engineering, operations, UX, communications and other expert perspectives can be introduced only when the question requires them.
