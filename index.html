04
The A–E protocol

Five movements.
One deliberation.

A–E is the visible rhythm of the CONCLAIVE method. The user does not operate these stages manually; the system orchestrates them dynamically according to the question.

A
Assemble

Determine what the question requires. Bring the right disciplines, evidence and perspectives to the table. Identify who would be dangerous to leave out.

B
Broaden

Expand the proposition beyond its initial assumptions. Surface constraints, possibilities, and stakeholder realities.

C
Challenge

Attack the proposition. Seek contradictions, unsupported assumptions, failure modes and reasons it might be wrong.

D
Deliberate

Allow competing positions to interact. Disagreement is a valid output. Independent disciplines can clash, reconcile, and test one another.

E
Emerge

Produce a defensible position — with uncertainty, unresolved questions and the human decision gate clearly preserved.

05
Governance

Governance lives
inside the process.

Governance is not a final compliance screen. It is expressed through the way a question is assembled, tested, challenged, recorded and returned to human authority.
